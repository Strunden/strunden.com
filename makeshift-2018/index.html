Make:Shift Ireland

2017
Speaker at Make:Shift Ireland
6 November 2017

Intro: From his early teens, Fabian Strunden designed and scripted engaging websites with a strong focus aesthetic and user experience. Studying industrial design at NCAD, Fabian began to challenge the disconnect of screen-based media and our physical environment.

As DAQRI's Dublin-based UX/UI design lead, Fabian is responsible for the user interface and user experience design of DAQRI's Visual Operating System™ featured on the DAQRI Smart Glasses™. In close collaboration with the engineering teams, he ideates next-generation industrial augmented reality experiences that revolutionise the way workers learn, communicate and access information.
Don’t miss Fabian speaking at #MakeShiftIreland on Monday, November 6th at The Helix, Dublin 9. Hosted by the Design & Crafts Council of Ireland and Crafts Council UK.
