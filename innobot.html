Innobot

Interactive Design Commission

2015
Goal
In 2015 I was commissioned to build an Interactive installation for visitors of the Innovation Showcase organised by Enterprise Ireland. 
The logo of the event resembled the buttons from the 80s toy Simon Says. Based on this I developed a large multi player installation
using Processing to present sound and visuals as well as an Arduino to detect the physical press of the buttons and. The game included audio and visual cues that where presented on an in build flat screen and speakers. 
The installation titled Innobot was well received during the show offered visitors a fun and memorable experience.
