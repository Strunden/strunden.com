Weightless Bricks 
Exhibited as part of Invisible Landscapes: Imagination (Act III) at the Royal Academy of Arts London
2 February - 1 April 2019

Weightless Bricks is a site-specific mixed reality experience created by Soft Bodies in collaboration with Fabian Strunden (UX Coding) and Kevin Pollard (Sound Design). It speculates on the imaginary potential of future virtual work environments.

Experienced through a virtual-reality headset, viewers will adopt the first-person perspective of a worker, led through a series of virtual spaces and blended with some of the physical objects presented in the display. The viewers will explore a hybrid scenario where physical architecture has become fully entangled with a digital world. This is a project proposing different scenarios for creative production in the virtual space in the near future.

Virtual Reality Partner: HTC Vive

More on: https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/event/weightless-bricks-soft-bodies-invisible-landscapes-architecture-programme-experience-virtual-reality
